S-Horizon

Use shz for lifecycle operations.
Configure S-Horizon in the dashboard or
through Collie.

Purpose
  Stop S-Horizon services safely.

Usage

shz stop [service]

Details
  An omitted service selects all
    services.
  The resident waits up to three
    minutes for active work.
  A stop remains in effect after login
    and computer restart.

Services
  resident
  dashboard
  agentsview

Options

  --mc-home <absolute-path>
    Select the deployment home.

  --no-color
    Disable color.

  --help
    Show help.
