S-Horizon

Use shz for lifecycle operations.
Configure S-Horizon in the dashboard or through Collie.

Purpose
  Show recent service logs.

Usage

shz logs [service] [--tail N]

Details
  An omitted service selects the resident.
  Show 100 recent records and exit.
  Use --tail N for 1 through 1000 records.

Services
  resident
  dashboard
  agentsview

Options

  --mc-home <absolute-path> Select the deployment home.
  --no-color                Disable color.
  --help                    Show help.
